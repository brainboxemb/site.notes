# Outdoor Furniture

This page contains ideas and building plans for outdoor furniture.

## Outdoor Sofa

[How to Build an Outdoor Sofa](https://www.woodshopdiaries.com/how-to-build-an-outdoor-couch/) from Woodshop Diaries describes a wooden outdoor sofa designed for three standard seat cushions.

### Approximate Dimensions

| Measurement | Imperial | Metric |
|---|---:|---:|
| Width | 77½ inches | 197 cm |
| Depth | 35 inches | 89 cm |
| Height | 29½ inches | 75 cm |
| Seat cushion size | 24 × 24 inches | 61 × 61 cm |

The design mainly uses 4×4 and 2×4 lumber. Assembly uses pocket-hole screws, exterior wood screws, timber screws and exterior-rated wood glue.

Printable building plans are available through the article.

## Chunky Outdoor Chair

[DIY Chunky Outdoor Chair](https://www.woodshopdiaries.com/diy-chunky-outdoor-chair/) is a matching outdoor chair from the same furniture series.

The chair uses a similar chunky wooden construction but is narrower and does not require the centre support used in the sofa.

The project uses common lumber sizes and a relatively small set of tools:

- Mitre saw
- Drill or driver
- Pocket-hole jig
- Sander

Printable building plans are available through the article.
